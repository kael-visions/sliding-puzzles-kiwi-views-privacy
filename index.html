Privacy Policy for Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views

Effective Date: October 2026

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views is a sliding puzzle game, playable offline, featuring scenic locations, landscapes, and views from across New Zealand. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not automatically collect, store, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app does not require users to create an account, log in, or provide personal information.

The app does not include advertising or analytics services. The only information Kael Visions receives is what you choose to send when you contact us (see "Contacting Support" below).

Local Data Storage

Game progress, unlocked artwork, avatars, rewards, bonus puzzles, and user preferences are stored locally on the user's device only. The app does not upload this information to external servers or cloud services.

Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or changing devices may result in permanent loss of locally stored game progress and preferences.

Sharing Content

The app includes an optional sharing feature that allows users to share completed artwork using other applications installed on their device. Sharing is initiated by the user and is handled through the device's available sharing functions.

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not track, log, or store content shared by users.

Contacting Support

If you use the Contact Us feature in the app, the app prepares an email in your device's email app. The email may include the details you enter (such as your name, email address, and message) together with technical information that helps diagnose problems, such as the app name and version and your device's manufacturer, model, and operating system version.

Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email, and you can edit or remove any of the details before doing so. If you do send it, Kael Visions receives the email and uses the information only to respond to your message and to help resolve issues. Emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. You may contact us at any time to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Notifications

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views may offer optional local notifications, such as a reminder to return to the game after a period of not playing. Notifications are only enabled where the required device permission has been granted.

Local notifications are scheduled and processed on the user's device. The app does not use a remote notification server for these notifications and does not collect personal information for notification purposes.

Where the device requires permission, the app asks once, after you complete your first puzzle. Users may decline notification permission when prompted or disable notifications at any time through their device settings.

In-App Purchases

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game, and may offer additional optional content in future updates. Payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not collect, store, or have access to users' payment or financial information. Payment processing is handled by Google Play in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Permissions

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views only requests permissions required for features provided by the app, such as local notifications, vibration feedback, or sharing content through other applications on the user's device.

Permissions are not used by the app to collect or transmit personal or sensitive user information.

Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play and uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases and Google Play's in-app update feature to offer app updates. Google may process certain technical, device, account, and transaction-related information as necessary to provide its services.

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not independently collect, store, or access users' payment or financial information. Information processed by Google is handled in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views is designed to be suitable for a general audience and does not require user accounts. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the app does not collect personal information directly, no personal data is knowingly collected from children through the app. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to Kael Visions by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

User Control

Users can control optional permissions, including notification permissions, through their device settings.

Users may reset game progress where supported by the app or remove the app from their device at any time using standard device controls.

International Users

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views may be distributed globally through Google Play. As the app does not directly collect personal information, it is designed to minimise the handling of personal data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to the app, changes to its features, or changes in applicable platform requirements. Any updates will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
